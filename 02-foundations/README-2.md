## Key Topics

### The CIA Triad

The three core principles of every security decision:

| Principle | Definition | Example Control |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| **Confidentiality** | Only authorized users can access data | Encryption, MFA, access controls |
| **Integrity** | Data is accurate and unaltered | Hashing, digital signatures |
| **Availability** | Systems accessible when needed | Backups, redundancy, uptime monitoring |

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) — 6 Functions

| Function | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Govern** | Set policies and risk priorities |
| **Identify** | Know your assets and risks |
| **Protect** | Implement safeguards |
| **Detect** | Monitor for security events |
| **Respond** | Act on detected incidents |
| **Recover** | Restore operations after an incident |

### Security Frameworks vs. Controls

- **Framework** = The overall structure/guidelines (e.g. NIST CSF, ISO 27001)
- **Control** = A specific action or safeguard to reduce risk

**Three Control Categories:**
- **Administrative** — Policies, procedures, training (e.g. least privilege policy)
- **Technical** — Firewalls, encryption, IDS, antivirus
- **Physical** — Locks, CCTV, badge readers

### Security Audit — Botium Toys Case Study

Conducted a mock internal audit for Botium Toys (a fictional company used in the Google curriculum). The audit assessed existing controls and compliance with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC standards.

**Key findings:**
- Missing: Least privilege, encryption, disaster recovery plan, IDS, backups
- Present: Firewall, antivirus, physical locks, CCTV
- Compliance gaps: PCI DSS (no encryption on card data), GDPR (data not classified)

**Recommendation summary:** Prioritize encryption and least privilege immediately; implement IDS and disaster recovery plan within 30 days.
